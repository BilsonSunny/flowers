Dear Aakriti,

Happy Birthday! 🌹

Some people you get to know right away. With you, it took me a while.

We worked together for months, but it wasn't until this year, when we all started hanging out, that I really get to know who you are. And I have to be honest: I have never met anyone as sweet as you. You have a sweet soul and the biggest heart, and you care about everyone around you, even when it costs you something.

Yes, you can be a little short-tempered sometimes (jk) but that's just you being real. It has never once taken away from how kind you are. If anything, it makes the kindness feel more genuine.

Here is what I hope for you this year:

I hope you get treated the way you treat everyone else, with patience, thoughtfulness and care.

I hope the people who lean on you remember to check in on you too.

I hope this year you get what you've been dreaming about.

And I hope every good thing you've given to others finds its way back to you.

You probably don't hear it enough, but you matter. The way you show up for people doesn't go unnoticed, even on the days you think it does.

I don't know what the future has in store, or where we'll all be five years from now. That's exactly why I wanted to give you something that lasts.

This isn't just a gift, it's a memory, and I hope that wherever life takes you, you'll open it someday and remember someone was really glad you were in their life.

Thank you for being exactly who you are.

cheers to 23.

best wishes & warm hugs
Bilson
